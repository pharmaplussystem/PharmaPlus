PHARMAPLUS V5 - START HERE

1. Open config.js in Notepad.
2. Replace PASTE_YOUR_SUPABASE_PROJECT_URL_HERE with your Supabase Project URL.
3. Replace PASTE_YOUR_SUPABASE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY_HERE with your Publishable key.
4. Save config.js.
5. In Supabase SQL Editor, run supabase_schema.sql.
6. Upload ALL files, including config.js, to your hosting/GitHub Pages.

Example:
window.PHARMAPLUS_SUPABASE_URL = "https://abcdefghijklmnop.supabase.co";
window.PHARMAPLUS_SUPABASE_KEY = "sb_publishable_xxxxxxxxx";

Do not use a service_role/secret key.
